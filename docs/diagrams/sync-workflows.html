A purchase updates two balances

A simulated package purchase issues service passes and earns 1 point per NT$1. It also creates an order and points record. It does not increase the visit count.

Attendance drives membership

Only a recorded check-in adds a visit. Plus starts at 0; Core at 10; Luxe at 20. The visit thresholds and benefits are provisional. Birthday benefits are previews, with no automatic issue.

02

A reservation uses one pass

The app rechecks availability and pass eligibility when the member confirms. The cancellation decision below applies when a member asks to cancel an existing reservation.

What the booking check includes

  • A future session within the next 30 days, with capacity available.
  • No duplicate or overlapping reservation.
  • A remaining pass for the same service, valid on the session date.
  • Use the selected Wallet pass, or the earliest-expiring eligible pass.

Cancellation preserves the original expiry

Member cancellation is allowed at least 24 hours before the session. The cutoff is checked again at confirmation. Inside that window, the booking stays confirmed. Restoring a pass never extends its expiry.

The 24-hour window and 30 / 90 / 180-day purchase pass validity are provisional policy settings.

03

Points become a new wellness moment

Redemption deducts points and creates a linked wallet record. Service rewards and guest vouchers have different ways of being used.

Service rewards

Yoga and Contrast Therapy rewards create one bookable pass. The linked reward becomes reserved when booked and used after a staff-confirmed check-in. Cancelling an eligible reservation restores the pass with the same expiry.

Guest rewards

A guest reward creates a voucher, rather than a member service pass. Front desk or admin validate the code and mark it used once. Reward prices and the 30-day validity remain provisional.

05 / Development scope

A working browser demo.
Production services come next.

This document describes the local implementation as inspected on 18 October 2026. It is a workflow reference, not an indication that the production integrations below already exist.

  • Data: wallets, sessions, orders and attendance persist in this browser's local storage. Accounts share demo records in that browser, with no backend or multi-device database.
  • Identity: sign-in and role checks are local demo behavior. Production authentication and server-side authorization still need implementation.
  • Transactions: purchases and reservations are simulated. No real payment collection, external booking service or live inventory integration is connected.
  • Messages and benefits: verification, password recovery and notifications do not send real messages. Birthday benefits are previews. Finalize provisional policy values before production.
